\section{Presentation Letter}


I am an AI Engineer and solution architect who designs, builds, and ships production LLM systems end to end, from shaping the solution with clients in pre-sales through architecture, implementation, cloud deployment, and knowledge transfer. My focus is the layers that decide whether an LLM system works in production: evaluation, retrieval and data design, and compliance. I build evaluation harnesses that show where accuracy is lost, use them to drive architecture and roadmap decisions, and benchmark model, cost, and latency trade-offs up front.

I have worked remotely with US companies delivering agentic AI to clients in healthcare, travel, media, and real estate: HIPAA-compliant document pipelines, multimodal document automation integrated with ERPs, NL2SQL and RAG assistants, and real-time voice agents that handle calls and operate web applications. Before that, I spent years in industrial AI in Brazil, taking ML from prototype to production: predictive maintenance and MLOps on AWS for wind turbines, and edge ML, computer vision, and IoT products.

I am looking to lead AI engineering and system architecture: setting technical direction, making trade-offs explicit, working closely with clients and stakeholders, and raising the engineering bar of the teams I work with.
